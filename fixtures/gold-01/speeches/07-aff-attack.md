> **このスピーチが Gold Dataset で最も難しい区間である。**
>
> 条項 2.1.4 は、肯定アタックで**直前の否定アタック⑤への再反論を禁止**する。
> ただし例外として、**否定立論③にアタックに該当するものが含まれていた場合**は、

>
> したがって検出器は、反論の**語調**ではなく**参照先のスピーチ**を辿らなければならない。
> 「③ で言われたこと」への反論は正当、「⑤ で言われたこと」への反論は違反である。
>

>

Thank you, chairperson. My name is Ken, and I will attack the negative's disadvantages.

I begin with their security disadvantage, and I begin with uniqueness.

Mr. Rui told us that the health declaration is collected at the time of application. He then agreed, in questioning, that forty percent of the world's countries enter Meridia today without any application. So forty percent of arrivals already reach our border with no declaration. The harm the negative describes is not created by our plan. It is the situation we are already in. What our plan changes is the paperwork, not the exposure.

Second, on their link. They gave you nine hundred refusals for a criminal record. When we asked how many of those nine hundred would have committed a crime in Meridia, Mr. Rui told us the report does not say. A refusal is not a prevented crime. They have a number of refusals and they are asking you to read it as a number of prevented harms. That step is not in their evidence.

Now their community disadvantage.

Their whole causal chain runs through one claim: that new visitors concentrate in Verano. Mr. Rui told us that claim is not in the council report. It is their reasoning. Against that, our plan's own logic sends short notice travellers to the coast, because the coast is where capacity exists.

And on uniqueness again. The cathedral stairway wore eleven millimetres over a decade. Mr. Rui agreed that Meridia had a visa requirement during that entire decade. The wear they describe happened under the system they are defending.

Mr. Rui said in his constructive that our exchange benefit cannot be measured, and therefore cannot be weighed. I answer that here. A benefit that has no single number is not a benefit of zero. Our schools already run exchange programmes and they already fail to run them because of the three week window. That failure is measurable, and Mr. Rui did not contest it.

Ms. Hana said a moment ago that our eight hundred dollar figure is a number we made ourselves. Let me answer that now. The figure is conservative. Even at half that amount, the industry is worth more than the six weeks she describes, and she has not shown that the six weeks would return.

For these reasons their disadvantages do not stand. Thank you.
