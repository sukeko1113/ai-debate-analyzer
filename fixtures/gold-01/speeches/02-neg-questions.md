# ② Questions from the Negative (2 min)

- **Questioner**: N4 (Emi) / **Responder**: A1 (Mina)
- **Planted**: なし（意図的にクリーン）
- 目的: ⑤ 否定アタックの材料を集める。特に Solera の類推と、根拠のない一人当たり支出額

> **ここに違反を仕込まない理由。** 質疑は誤検出が起きやすい区間である。
> 短い応酬・言い直し・沈黙が多く、「何かおかしい」と判定されやすい。
> クリーンな質疑を置くことで、Precision の測定に効く。

---

**Emi**: Thank you, chairperson. My name is Emi. Ms. Mina, you said Solera's arrivals grew thirty-four percent. Does Solera have the same population as Meridia?

**Mina**: Not exactly the same. Solera is a little larger.

**Emi**: How much larger?

**Mina**: I do not have that number with me.

**Emi**: Thank you. You also said Meridia and Solera share a similar hotel capacity. Is that from the white paper you cited?

**Mina**: That is our own understanding of the region.

**Emi**: So it is not in the white paper.

**Mina**: The white paper covers arrivals. The hotel comparison is ours.

**Emi**: Thank you. Next question. You said each visitor spends about eight hundred dollars. Where does that figure come from?

**Mina**: It is a commonly used figure in our tourism sector.

**Emi**: Is it in the Tourism Authority statistics?

**Mina**: I believe something like it is, yes.

**Emi**: You believe. Do you know?

**Mina**: I did not read out that specific line, no.

**Emi**: Thank you. Moving on. You presented three advantages. If you had to keep only one, which would you keep?

**Mina**: I would keep tourism revenue.

**Emi**: So regional employment is less important than tourism revenue?

**Mina**: They are connected. The jobs come from the revenue.

**Emi**: Then is regional employment a separate advantage, or part of the first one?

**Mina**: I would say it is a separate outcome of the same plan.

**Emi**: Thank you. Last question. Your plan permits stays of up to ninety days. Does anything in your plan check who is entering?

**Mina**: Passport control still operates.

**Emi**: But nothing before they arrive.

**Mina**: Nothing before they arrive, correct.

**Emi**: Thank you. That is all.
