# ⑫ Negative Summary (3 min)

- **Speaker**: N4 (Emi)
- **Target**: 約 420 words（3分で 140 wpm）
- **Planted**: violation **#1** `new_argument`

> **#1 の設計**: 条項 4.2.2 は、総括で初めて提出された「新しい議論」をジャッジが無視すると定める。
> 明らかに該当するのは、**相手の Advantage の証明に対する新しいアタックの証拠**が
> 総括で出た場合である。
>
> ここでは、**⑤ の否定アタックに一度も出てこなかった攻撃**を総括で持ち出す。
> AD1 の effect に対する新しい攻撃であり、しかも**新しい証拠（航空座席供給の制約）**を伴う。
> 相手には反論の機会が一切ない位置に置いてある。
>
> **T1 との違いが、この Gold Dataset の核心である。**
>
> | | ⑪ の T1 | ⑫ の #1 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 出したもの | 比較の観点（可逆性・被害範囲） | AD1 effect への新しい攻撃 |
> | 新しい証拠 | 無し | **有り**（航空座席） |
> | 参照先 | すべて既出の AD/DA | ⑤ にも ③ にも無い論点 |
> | 判定 | **正当な比較。フラグを立てない** | **新しい議論。フラグを立てる** |
>
> 「総括で初めて出たか」だけを見ると両方に立つ。
> **「新しい論点か、既出論点の比較か」を見なければ区別できない。**

> **もう一点**: ⑫ は ⑨ 肯定ディフェンスへの再々反論が許される唯一の場所である
> （条項 2.1.6 の注記）。ここでは正当な範囲でそれを行う。

Thank you, chairperson. My name is Emi, and I will give the final speech of this round.

Mr. Tomo told you where each argument ended. I agree with him about two of them and I disagree about two.

I agree that their second advantage is reduced. He conceded the final step himself. And I agree that our concentration claim in Verano is reasoning rather than a citation. I said so when I was asked.

Where I disagree is on their first advantage, and on our first disadvantage.

Let me take their advantage first. Ms. Sara told you in defence that we conceded the direction of the effect when we argued about size. That is not what we said. And there is a reason the size cannot be what they claim, which the round has not yet examined. Meridia has four international air routes. Airline seat supply into Meridia grew by two percent last year. A visa is not what limits arrivals when the seats do not exist. Solera has eleven routes. That is the difference between the two countries, and it is why the thirty-four percent does not transfer. Their entire economic case assumes travellers who have no way to get here.

Now our disadvantage. The affirmative's answer, repeated three times, is that the visa did not stop the 2019 outbreak. That is true and it has always been true. Our disadvantage was never that the visa stops outbreaks. It is that the health declaration tells our hospitals where an arrival has been. Mr. Ken conceded that the declaration has value. Ms. Sara did not answer the declaration at all. She answered the visa. Our argument is still standing because they have spent three speeches attacking something we did not claim.

On the comparison, Mr. Tomo offered you range. One town against many villages. But his villages only benefit if travellers arrive, and I have just shown you what limits arrivals.

So what is left? An advantage whose size they conceded, whose mechanism cannot deliver, and a supporting reason they abandoned. Against a health harm they never addressed, and an irreversible harm to a town already at its limit.

Vote negative. Thank you.
