# ⑧ Questions from the Negative (2 min)

- **Questioner**: N3 (Yuji) / **Responder**: A2 (Ken)
- **Planted**: なし（意図的にクリーン）

> 条項 2.1.9 により、アタック後の質疑では**原則として相手のアタックについて**質問する。
> ここでは ⑦ の内容だけを扱い、立論には戻らない。

---

**Yuji**: Thank you, chairperson. My name is Yuji. Mr. Ken, you said forty percent already arrive without a declaration. Does that mean the remaining sixty percent carries no value?

**Ken**: It means the harm is not unique to our plan.

**Yuji**: My question is about value. Is a declaration from sixty percent of arrivals worth nothing?

**Ken**: It has some value. Our point is that it is partial already.

**Yuji**: So you accept it has value.

**Ken**: Partial value, yes.

**Yuji**: Thank you. Second question. You said a refusal is not a prevented crime. Is a refusal evidence of nothing at all?

**Ken**: It is evidence that someone had a record.

**Yuji**: And is someone with a serious criminal record more likely to offend than someone without one?

**Ken**: That is not established in their evidence.

**Yuji**: I am asking what you believe, not what the report says.

**Ken**: I would not accept it without a figure.

**Yuji**: Thank you. Third question. You said the coast has capacity. What is your evidence for the capacity of the coast?

**Ken**: Our first speaker described the coastal villages losing population.

**Yuji**: Losing population is not the same as having hotel capacity. Do you have a figure?

**Ken**: Not a specific figure, no.

**Yuji**: Thank you. Last question. You answered our attack on your eight hundred dollar figure just now, in your attack speech. Was that answer part of your attack on our disadvantages?

**Ken**: It was a response to what was said.

**Yuji**: Thank you. That is all.
