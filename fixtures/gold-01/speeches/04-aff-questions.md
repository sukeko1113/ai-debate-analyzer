# ④ Questions from the Affirmative (2 min)

- **Questioner**: A4 (Tomo) / **Responder**: N1 (Rui)
- **Planted**: なし（意図的にクリーン）
- 目的: ⑦ 肯定アタックの材料を集める。特に DA1 の固有性と DA2 の因果

---

**Tomo**: Thank you, chairperson. My name is Tomo. Mr. Rui, you said nine hundred applicants were refused for a serious criminal record. Were all nine hundred trying to enter as tourists?

**Rui**: The report covers tourist visa applications.

**Tomo**: Does the report say how many of the nine hundred would have committed a crime in Meridia?

**Rui**: It does not say that.

**Tomo**: So we do not know whether any of them would have.

**Rui**: We know they had a record.

**Tomo**: Thank you. Second question. The forty percent of countries that already enter without a visa. Do they submit a health declaration?

**Rui**: I would have to check.

**Tomo**: Your own evidence says the declaration is submitted at the time of application. If there is no application, is there a declaration?

**Rui**: For those countries, presumably not.

**Tomo**: So forty percent of the world already arrives without one, today.

**Rui**: That is a fair reading.

**Tomo**: Thank you. Third question. You said visitors go to the one place they have heard of. What is your evidence that new visitors choose Verano rather than the coast?

**Rui**: It is how short-notice travel works.

**Tomo**: Is that in the council report?

**Rui**: The council report covers Verano itself.

**Tomo**: So the claim that new visitors concentrate in Verano is your reasoning, not the report.

**Rui**: The report shows the pressure. The reasoning is ours.

**Tomo**: Thank you. Last question. The stairway wore eleven millimetres in a decade. Was there a visa requirement during that decade?

**Rui**: There was.

**Tomo**: Thank you. That is all.
