Thank you, chairperson. My name is Rui, and I will present the negative constructive speech.

The affirmative asks you to remove the only check that happens before a traveller arrives. We will show two disadvantages: a security and public health disadvantage, and a disadvantage to our local communities.

My first disadvantage is security and public health.

Let me start with the present situation. Today, the tourist visa is not only a piece of paper. It is a screening step. According to the Meridia Ministry of Interior Security Report of 2024, our consulates refused three thousand four hundred applications last year. Of those refusals, nine hundred were refused because the applicant had a serious criminal record in another country. The same report states that every applicant submits a health declaration at the time of application, and that this declaration is the only health information our border service receives before arrival.

Now the effect of their plan. If the visa disappears, the screening disappears with it. The affirmative admitted in questioning that nothing in their plan checks anyone before they arrive. Passport control at the airport can confirm identity, but it cannot run the background check that a consulate runs over three weeks. Nine hundred people who were stopped last year would not be stopped next year. And no health declaration would reach our border service at all.

The importance is serious and it is not hypothetical. In 2019 our region experienced an outbreak that closed our schools for six weeks. Our public health service traced the first cases to arriving travellers. The health declaration system was introduced after that outbreak, precisely so that it would not happen again. Our hospitals were not able to isolate patients quickly because they did not know where the arrivals had travelled from. The affirmative plan removes it. When the affirmative counts nine hundred and sixty million dollars of tourism, they are counting money. We are counting the six weeks that our children did not go to school.

My second disadvantage is pressure on our local communities.

The present situation is that Verano is already at its limit. According to the Verano City Council Report of 2024, the old town receives four hundred thousand visitors a year against a resident population of eleven thousand. The same report records that resident complaints about noise, waste, and street congestion rose by sixty percent over three years, and that the council has already restricted new short-term rental licences.

The effect of the affirmative plan is a surge that Verano cannot absorb. Remove the visa, and the visitors who come are the ones who decide at short notice. Those visitors do not book the coastal train that the affirmative described. They go to the one place they have heard of, and that place is Verano. When demand rises in a town of eleven thousand people, landlords convert homes into rentals, rents rise, and residents leave. The council report records that two hundred families have already moved out of the old town in the last five years.

The importance is that this damage cannot be undone. Meridia Daily, on November the twelfth, 2024, reported that the stone stairways of the Verano cathedral have worn by eleven millimetres in a decade, and that the restoration authority describes the wear as permanent. A hotel can be built. A resident who has left does not come back, and a worn stone is not restored.

Before I close, I want to say one thing about the affirmative's second advantage. They claim an educational benefit from international exchange. They gave us no measurement of that benefit. They gave us no study, no survey, and no figure. A benefit that cannot be measured cannot be weighed against six weeks of closed schools.

For these reasons, the disadvantages of this plan outweigh its advantages, and the resolution should be negated. Thank you.
