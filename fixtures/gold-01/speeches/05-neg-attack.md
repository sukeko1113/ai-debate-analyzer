Thank you, chairperson. My name is Hana, and I will attack the affirmative's advantages.

I have three attacks. The first two go to their first advantage. The third goes to their second advantage.

My first attack is on their causal claim. They say Solera's thirty-four percent proves Meridia will grow. In questioning they told us Solera is larger, and they could not tell us by how much. They told us the hotel comparison is their own view and not in the white paper they cited. So what remains of the analogy? One number from one country, and a claim of similarity that they cannot support. If Solera is larger, it absorbs a surge that Meridia cannot. The analogy does not carry the weight they place on it.

My second attack is on their importance. They gave you a figure of nine hundred and sixty million dollars. Where did it come from? They multiplied one point two million visitors by eight hundred dollars. Where does eight hundred dollars come from? They told us it is a commonly used figure. They told us they did not read out the line. So the largest number in their case is a number they made themselves, from a figure they cannot source. Remove that number and the advantage does not disappear, but its size does. You cannot weigh what has not been shown.

My third attack goes to international exchange. They claim an educational benefit. Ask yourself what they proved. They proved that visitors would come. They did not prove that any student meets any visitor. They did not prove that meeting a visitor changes anything. A tourist who spends four days in a coastal hotel does not enter a classroom. Between arrivals and education there are three steps, and the affirmative proved none of them.

Now let me turn to what our disadvantage means, because it answers their weighing.

Doctor Alvarez says that the health declaration is the single most useful piece of information the border service receives, because it tells us where an arrival has been in the previous fourteen days. Without it, our hospitals are in the position they were in in 2019, treating patients without knowing where the infection came from.

The affirmative will tell you that forty percent of countries already arrive without a declaration. That is true, and it is exactly why the remaining sixty percent matters. You do not remove the lock on the second door because the first one is open. You fix the first door.

So where does the round stand? Their first advantage rests on an analogy they cannot support and a number they made up. Their second advantage rests on three unproven steps. Our disadvantage rests on a government report, a named expert, and an outbreak that closed our schools six weeks.

The resolution should be negated. Thank you.
